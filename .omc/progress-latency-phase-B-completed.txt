### Key learnings
- Updating the transcript filter's contract forced updating existing state-machine tests that used short transcripts. Wake-word prefix ("Гава, ...") is the cleanest way to bypass LAT-B1 filters in tests that care about decider state transitions, not filter behavior.
- Speculative context timing: 200ms wait-for-in-flight-speculation balances "don't block user" with "don't duplicate work". For most utterances (>500ms speech) the speculation completes during VAD stop_secs anyway.
