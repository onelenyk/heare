### Key learnings
- Pipecat 0.0.108 uses deprecated vad_analyzer/turn_analyzer params on transport — they still work but emit warnings. Migration to user_turn_strategies is a separate refactor.
- Streaming MP3 → ffmpeg stdin requires `proc.stdin.drain()` between writes to avoid backpressure deadlocks.
- ffmpeg with very small inputs (silence) buffers everything before flushing — use a tone (≥1s) to test streaming behavior.
- patch.dict on sys.modules + lazy `import` inside the function works correctly for testing edge_tts mocks IF the module isn't already cached.
- HeartbeatTask floor of 1.0s prevented short-interval testing; lowered to 0.01s.
