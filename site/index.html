01 What it is

It listens, decides, answers.

A voice assistant for macOS that behaves like a conversation partner, not a command in a terminal.

It listens, decides for itself whether it is being spoken to, answers aloud and falls silent the moment you talk over it. No button — the conversation starts with words.

It lives in the menu bar. Its face shows what it is doing right now — you can see it without asking.

  • waiting quiet until it is spoken to
  • listening holds your phrase
  • thinking decides what to answer
  • speaking answers aloud
  • off does not listen or answer

02 Bodies

One mind, many bodies.

One brain. As many bodies as there are: a Mac, a page in any browser, a small panel on the desk. Each part is its own process with its own key.

03 What it can do

Six things it does alongside you.

01

voice

Listens, decides for itself whether it is being spoken to, answers aloud. Falls silent when you talk over it.

02

hands

Two kinds. Native hands act on your bodies — an OLED panel, a light, a pin — in a second or two. CLI hands give the work to an agent on the Mac: opencode or Claude Code, code, files, commands, web searches.

03

eyes

Sees which app is in front and whether something is playing.

04

sources

A short “Now” line: the weather now and for the next few hours. Open-Meteo, no key. It is true only while it lasts.

05

memory

Conversations, facts and reminders in ~/.heare. Facts are pulled from the talk on their own, every 5 minutes. Keys live in the Keychain.

06

panel

A small ESP32 with a 72×40 OLED on the desk. It shows what the brain decided.

04 How it works

From a word to an action.

The brain does not keep everything inside. It takes what the parts bring, decides, and passes the work on.

01

A part knocks

Voice, eyes, a page or a panel — each on its own, with its own key.

02

The brain decides

Whether it is being spoken to, what to answer, and who gets the work.

03

The hands act

Native hands — at once, on the bodies. CLI hands — through an agent on the Mac.

04

The pult shows

What happened: state, conversation, memory, parts.

05 Privacy

Private by default.

Memory and keys live on the Mac. Remote calls go only to the models you chose.

Memory

Conversations, facts and reminders live in ~/.heare on your Mac. Not in someone else’s cloud.

Keys

In the Keychain. Written once and never shown again.

Remote models

Only what is needed: DeepSeek thinks, Groq hears.

Weather

Open-Meteo, no key. Now and for the next few hours; only while it lasts.

06 Limits

What does not work yet.

Better to know this before installing. The limits are not hidden in small print.

!

From a phone — https only.

The page works in any browser, but the microphone needs https. heare does not ship a tunnel or a domain yet.

!

The CLI agent cannot touch the bodies.

Only native hands call the parts’ skills. The agent can do code, files and commands — but not a light or the panel.

!

The app is unsigned.

After every macOS update it asks for the microphone again.

!

The brain can live elsewhere, but not be found from outside.

It can live on Linux or in Docker. TLS and a public address are not there out of the box.
